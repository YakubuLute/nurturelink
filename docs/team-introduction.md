# NurtureLink — Team Introduction

**Hackathon:** UNICEF AI for Nurturing Care Hackathon (KOICA / MEST StartUp Lab)
**Submission Deadline:** 11 August 2026

---

## Team Overview

NurtureLink is built by a two-person team combining 7+ years of production software engineering with formal health information management expertise and hands-on public health field experience in Ghana. Our backgrounds are complementary by design: one of us builds the system; the other ensures it reflects how health data actually moves in a Ghanaian community health setting.

## What We Are Building Together

Yakubu builds the system. Leticia validates it. Specifically:

- She reviewed the client registration fields against real CHPS recording forms and identified the gaps now being addressed (ANC folder number, CWC card number, gravida/parity, LMP, caregiver name).
- She confirmed the clinical thresholds (MUAC, Hb, weight-for-age Z-scores) against WHO/GHS guidance.
- She is the team's primary contact for the field validation conversation with a working CHO — the human grounding that an AI-assisted tool requires before it can credibly claim to serve frontline workers.
- She will review all feeding plan outputs for clinical appropriateness before the demo.
